Help · How to Use the Planner

Quick Screenshot Tour

Follow the arrows. No stress.

A quick visual tour of the four screens students actually use: set your programme, plan your semesters, enter grades, then check graduation readiness.

Do not skip this

The recommended plan is just a starter.

It only fills the schedule. Your graduation status and CGPA need a grade entered for every planned course — the plan alone does not make you eligible.

Four Steps

From zero to graduation-ready

Step 1

Pick the correct programme first

On the Planner (or Settings), use the top dropdowns to choose your programme, specialization, English pathway and language. If this part is wrong, every prerequisite and credit check after it will be wrong too.

Open the Planner
Step 2

Build the semester plan

Load the recommended structure for a fast start, then shape it: add courses from each semester's dropdown, and drag any course to move it to another semester until the plan makes sense for you.

Open the Planner
Step 3

Enter your grades

As you finish each course, set its grade in the dropdown on the right of the row. Your semester GPA, the CGPA ring and the Graduation Checklist all update instantly — no recalculating by hand.

See CGPA Simulation
Step 4

Check graduation readiness

Open the Graduation Checklist. Read the big status first — it tells you whether you can graduate yet — then work down the requirements and clear each unmet one, calmly, one by one.

Open the Checklist
🎉

Bonus shortcut — instant Dean's List

On the Planner, each semester box has a “🎉 Simulate a Dean's List semester for me!” button. It auto-fills that semester with top grades so you can preview your best-case GPA and CGPA in one click — no keying grades in one by one.
