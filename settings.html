Help & Support

Why is a course locked?
A course locks when a prerequisite is missing or failed, a minimum-credit threshold isn't met, or it's only offered in odd/even semesters. The dropdown shows the exact reason.
How do English pathways work?
Your MUET band sets your pathway (1–3 or Exempted), which determines the English Core courses you must complete. Change it above; the audit updates accordingly.
Can I change my foreign language?
Yes. For Economics and Agribusiness, switching language family automatically re-maps any placed language courses to the equivalent in the new family.
Is my data private?
Completely. Everything lives in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded, and the whole site works offline.

About

Built by SEFB, for SEFB students.

Developed by two SEFB academic staff, Prof. Dr. Soon Jan Jan and Assoc. Prof. Dr. Mukhriz Izraf Azman Aziz, the SEFB Degree Planner is an interactive web application that helps SEFB students plan their academic progression, simulate their CGPA, and check their graduation readiness.

Real UUM curriculum and grading with full prerequisite enforcement — self-contained and offline-ready.
